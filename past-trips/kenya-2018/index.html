Kenya · June 2018

Faith made practical in Kenya.

Teaching, building, friendship, and wonder carried us from Maua to the open landscapes of Kenya.

The journey

Serving with our words, hands, and presence.

Our 2018 journey took us through Nairobi, Maua, Samburu, Maasai Mara, and Lake Nakuru. Each place offered a different view of Kenya and a different invitation to listen, learn, serve, and receive the welcome of the people we met.

In Maua, we taught devotionals and shared encouragement with the community. We also worked alongside local partners at a hospital compound, helping build a new perimeter wall. The wall was a practical safety project intended to protect patients and staff by preventing unauthorized access to the hospital grounds.

Beyond the construction work, we met and played with local children. Laughter, games, conversation, and time together became some of the journey’s most meaningful moments. They reminded us that service is not only measured by a completed project, but also by whether we slow down enough to notice people and share life with them.

Several safaris brought us face to face with giraffes, elephants, lions, antelope, ostriches, topi, hyenas, and broad herds moving across the landscape. Those experiences gave us time to rest, reflect, and marvel at the beauty and diversity of God’s creation.

Kenya 2018 brought ministry and discovery together: devotionals offered in faith, a wall built for safety, friendships formed through play and presence, and a renewed sense of wonder carried home.
