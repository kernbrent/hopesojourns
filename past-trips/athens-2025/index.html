A lesson in seeing people.

A formative journey that reshaped how service, dignity, and humility are understood.

The story

Changed by a simple request.

During homeless outreach in Athens, Brent met a woman who asked for a red juice box. When only a blue one was available, her preference initially seemed unimportant. Later, a teammate’s simple reminder—she wanted a red one—revealed something deeper.

The moment became a lesson in dignity. People are not projects, problems, or tasks to complete. Each person carries preferences, worth, and a story. The journey changed Brent’s posture from going to “fix” people toward listening, honoring, and serving with humility.

The Christian Steps archive preserves this reflection along with scenes from the Acropolis, Mars Hill, and a local church supporting refugees.
