The journey

Listening before leading.

Our team served alongside churches and ministry partners caring for refugees, people experiencing homelessness, and vulnerable neighbors throughout Athens. Practical help mattered, but relationships gave that work its meaning.

We spent time with Sofia and Nikos of Hellenic Ministries of Athens, whose local knowledge helped our visiting team understand the community more deeply. We also gathered with the Albanian Church of Athens and met people faithfully serving across cultures and languages.

She wanted a red one.

During homeless outreach, a woman asked for a red juice box. We had a blue one available, and the difference initially seemed unimportant. Then a teammate quietly reminded us: she wanted a red one.

That small request became a lasting lesson. People are not tasks to finish. They have preferences, stories, and God-given dignity. Humble service slows down long enough to notice the person in front of us.
