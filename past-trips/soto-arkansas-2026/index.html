Preparing the way to serve.

A four-day visit built around listening, learning, prayer, and careful preparation.

The purpose

Planning future Hope Sojourns work teams.

A planning team traveled to Shepherd of the Ozarks (SOTO) to see the ministry firsthand, meet the people serving there, and understand its facilities, work, and practical needs.

The visit helped determine how and when Hope Sojourns can begin sending work teams to SOTO. Time was spent walking the grounds, discussing potential projects, and considering the skills, tools, team sizes, and seasons that future service may require.

The goal is not simply to arrive with willing hands. It is to build relationships, understand the work that will truly help, and prepare volunteers to serve side by side with the people already carrying SOTO’s mission.
