YOUR FIELD GUIDE

The unexpected answer
is the treasure.

This is a common-knowledge discovery game. The question is easy to enter; the challenge is thinking beyond the first answer that comes to mind.

  1. Choose a destination. Build a passport one discovery at a time.
  2. Start with the obvious. Food, a familiar Bible name or a country you know can earn a stamp. Need a starting point? Open the free clue.
  3. Think beyond the obvious. For “What do people need for everyday life?”, food earns 35 points, love 85, and someone to talk to 100. Several answers can be valid. The points describe the game, not the importance of a need.
  4. Beat the clock. You have 25 seconds to start each question, with limited retry time and a three-second typing cushion (see Saved progress & privacy). If time runs out, you miss the question: no points or stamp. Skips and timeouts reveal a discovery before you continue.
  5. Reach your destination. Everyone arrives after seven questions, even with missed answers. A lower score may mean a layover. Seven correct answers earn a 150-point passport bonus. Your final answer score can add up to another 150 points, for a maximum of 1,000.

What does “less obvious” mean?

Our 35/50/65/85/100 point tiers are editorial estimates of likely answers within each prompt. They are not measured percentages. The aim is an easy answer most people could give, plus discoveries fewer people might think of. Thoughtful answers are welcome, and equivalent wording and clear spelling mistakes count.

New rounds include Bible and faith, geography and travel, and everyday life and caring for people. Questions about homelessness, refugees and exploitation focus on dignity, welcome and practical care. They do not ask players to diagnose situations or guess facts about vulnerable people.

The accepted answer bank is curated and cannot contain every reasonable idea. Selected open-ended questions can check unfamiliar ideas automatically. New accepted ideas earn provisional points and enter private editorial review. Factual questions keep their curated checks. Suggest an answer or equivalent phrase after any reveal. Game points have no cash value or trip credit.

ON YOUR DEVICE

Your place is saved here.

Biblillion saves your current daily round and practice round in this browser. Come back and pick up where you left off. A short first-party cookie marks your latest daily progress; your resumable round stays in browser storage. Anonymous result copies are recorded separately in MMT.

No name, email or account is needed. Anonymous game stats are recorded automatically when you answer, skip or run out of time. No sharing switch is required.

The private MMT dashboard receives canonical answers or skips, spelling-correction and timeout flags, destination, mode and scores. Names, emails, private reflections and persistent player identities are not requested. For all everyday-life/care questions and selected open-ended travel questions, an unfamiliar short answer is sent to Cloudflare AI for checking and saved in a private MMT answer-review queue, including unsuccessful or uncertain checks. Please enter an idea, not personal details. Known answers are checked here without AI. Counts describe recorded rounds, not unique people. Detailed results are kept for 180 days; anonymous totals may be retained longer. A temporary retry queue helps record results after a connection interruption.

Clearing your saved progress also clears stats waiting in this browser. It does not remove anonymous stats already recorded in MMT. Earlier unshared answers are not uploaded retroactively.

Each question starts at 25 seconds. A new unrecognized answer longer than four non-space characters adds up to five seconds, never above 15 seconds. Repeating the same answer adds nothing. If typing has begun before zero, a single three-second finishing cushion is available; answers submitted in that cushion receive no time bonus. The clock keeps running during automatic checking; an answer submitted in time can still be accepted when its check returns.

Scripture links open Bible Gateway. The correction link opens your email app only when chosen.

Private browsing, cleared browser data, another device, or disabled storage may prevent resuming. A completed daily journey renews after midnight in America/Chicago. An unfinished older journey stays available; Start today’s new discovery replaces it only when you choose. Practice keeps separate progress but shares question history with Daily discovery. All 100 current regular questions appear before repeats. The topic mix can vary near the end of that pool. Clearing progress also clears question history; another browser or device starts its own history.
