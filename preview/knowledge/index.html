Your privacy in this preview

Questions and feedback stay in session storage in this browser tab. They are not sent to Hope Sojourns or OpenAI. Do not enter personal, financial, medical, or traveler information. Shared-device users can see this tab’s demo records. Closing the tab usually clears them; browser session restoration can retain them. Use Clear my demo to remove them explicitly.

Voice recording, web search, live AI, external messages, and cross-device analytics are disabled. Preview question limits are for demonstration, not server abuse protection.
