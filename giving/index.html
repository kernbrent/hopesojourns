Know the difference

How funds work.

Not every payment connected with a mission trip is a charitable contribution.

Give to the ministry

An unrestricted gift is received and controlled by Christian Steps Ministries and supports the ministry's overall mission. It does not reserve travel, purchase services, or provide a personal benefit.

Support a traveler

Support designated for a participant must follow the receiving organization's policies. The organization receiving charitable gifts must retain appropriate control over how those funds are used.

Pay for a trip

Trip payments may cover airfare, lodging, meals, insurance, transportation, ministry expenses, and coordination. Paying for travel or receiving services is different from making an unrestricted gift.

Hire Hope Sojourns

Churches, schools, ministries, or teams may purchase planning, training, internship, or coordination services. These service fees will be identified separately from charitable gifts and trip costs.

A simple promise: We will not call a payment a donation when it is actually paying for travel or services. Charitable gifts are received by Christian Steps Ministries, a registered 501(c)(3), and are tax-deductible to the extent allowed by law.
