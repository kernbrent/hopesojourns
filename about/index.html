Meet Brent

Experience that connects purpose with practical action.

Brent Kern brings 32 years of church service together with more than 28 years of corporate IT delivery and management experience. Across both settings, he has learned how to listen carefully, lead diverse teams, navigate complex challenges, and turn good intentions into practical next steps.

His ministry experience includes serving as a music pastor, interim pastor, children’s and youth director, and itinerant preacher. As the founder of Christian Steps Ministries, Brent continues to bring together Christian faith, teaching, service, and encouragement for people at different points in their journeys.

Brent’s background also includes music and performance, youth sports, and extensive domestic and international travel. Those experiences have placed him alongside people of many ages, cultures, professions, and perspectives—preparation that shapes the welcoming, adaptable, and relationship-centered approach of Hope Sojourns.

Travel has reinforced a simple conviction: the best service begins with humility. Hope Sojourns grows from Brent’s desire to help people cross cultures thoughtfully, learn from committed local leaders, and offer skills that meet real needs.
