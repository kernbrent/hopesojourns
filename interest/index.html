Interest first. Registration follows.

We will begin with a conversation about fit, timing, expectations, and current partner needs. When a particular trip is ready for registration, participants will receive a separate secure application.

Why two steps?

The interest form stays simple. The later registration process can carefully handle travel details, agreements, and other information that needs stronger privacy and review.
