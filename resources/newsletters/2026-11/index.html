A moment that stayed

The red juice box

A short reflection from Brent Kern

One evening in Athens, twelve of us were walking the streets with sandwiches, snacks, and juice boxes. Every few blocks, someone stopped to talk. I carried a canvas bag, ready to offer what we had brought.

A woman stood in front of me, speaking quickly. We couldn’t understand each other. I handed her food and a juice box. She accepted the food but pushed the drink back and pointed into my bag.

Our guide, Artur, explained: she wanted a red juice box. I looked again. Blue, blue, nothing else. Eventually, she took the sandwich and walked away. I stood there holding the drink, wondering why she couldn’t just be grateful.

Back at the apartment, I told the team what had happened. I thought I was sharing an unusual story. Amanda listened, then said, “Brent… she wanted a red juice box.”

I gave a little laugh. Of course I understood that. But Amanda didn’t move on. She said it again. In that pause, I began to understand what I had missed.

This woman had preferences. Wants. A life of her own. I had been so focused on giving her something that I had reduced her to a need I could meet. Her circumstances had not erased her right to want something different.

I had thought compassion began with doing, helping, or fixing. That evening taught me it begins earlier: actually seeing a person. I still carry that lesson into every journey.

That is the heart of Hope Sojourns, and I invite you to join us in reaching people and sharing the love of Jesus Christ. Come ready to give your time, energy, and attention—to listen to someone’s story, honor their dignity, and see a person loved by God, never an object on the street. Join us in Mexico this January, England this April, or Greece this July as we learn to see people more clearly and love them more faithfully.

What Hope Sojourns does

We bring willing people alongside trusted local ministries for Christian mission and service journeys. We listen to local leaders, contribute to work already underway, and make space for relationships and faith to grow.

We also help churches, teams and students explore group journeys and ministry internships. The goal is service that strengthens our partners and continues to shape us after we return.
