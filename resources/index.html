More than a reading list

Let the journey begin before you leave.

Mission begins long before a plane takes off, and its lessons should continue long after we return home. This collection brings together articles, sermons, devotionals, songs, videos, and other trusted resources that can help us prepare our hearts, understand the people and places we encounter, serve without assuming, and reflect on what God may be teaching us.

Some resources belong together. An article may grow into a sermon, a devotional may help you sit with the same idea more personally, and a song or book may offer another way into the conversation. Look for the Part of a collection label to follow those connections and explore a theme from more than one direction.
