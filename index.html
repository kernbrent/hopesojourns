Our approach

Meaningful service begins with relationship.

Mission journeys, internships, and corporate experiences begin with the priorities of people already serving their community. We listen, prepare, and contribute under local leadership.

Try this now

Begin with better questions.

Before choosing a destination, learn to notice the people, leadership, strengths, and priorities already present in a community.

Read “Do You See Me?”
  • Who is already serving faithfully?
  • What do local leaders say is actually needed?
  • What assumptions should I examine before arriving?
